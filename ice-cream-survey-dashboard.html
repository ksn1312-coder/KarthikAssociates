Social Media Influence
Key Insight: Social media advertising has minimal impact on purchase decisions (76% say no). Quality, taste, and traditional word-of-mouth matter far more than digital marketing for this demographic.
Strategic Takeaways
Quality Over Price: 48% prioritize quality as the main purchase driver, while only 2% care primarily about price. Premium positioning is validated.
Experience Matters: Ice cream parlours (44%) dominate over convenience channels. In-store experience and ambiance are crucial differentiators.
Flavor Innovation: 87% are willing to try exotic flavors. There's strong appetite for unique offerings beyond the traditional three.
Brand Landscape: Arun leads at 37%, but 23% buy from premium/specialty brands. Room exists for differentiated positioning.
